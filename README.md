# EV Specs & Sales Explorer

**Data Analytics with Tableau – Capstone Project**

**Author:** Princy Malviya, Medicaps University · **Team ID:** NA

A Tableau dashboard built on 199,999 cleaned electric vehicle records. It links sales by use case and manufacturing country with price, top speed, range, drivetrain, autonomy level and customer ratings.

## Key findings
- **Use cases:** Family Daily Use (620.5 M units) and Urban Commute (425.6 M) make up about two-thirds of EV sales.
- **Price:** average prices range from 2,555 USD (Two-Wheeler Scooter) to 274,543 USD (Electric Bus).
- **Ratings:** customer ratings are about 4.09 / 5 for every battery type.
- **Manufacturing:** India (160.0 M) and China (159.4 M) lead, but all 10 countries hold about 10% of sales.
- **Technology:** AWD dominates SUVs, pickups and sports cars and gives about 55 km more range; 27.5% of EVs offer Level 4–5 autonomy.
